I'm **Lakshman Shanmugam**, an AI/ML Engineer experienced in building LLM-powered applications, agentic workflows, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, and machine learning solutions across cybersecurity, Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC), and healthcare.

I work as a **Junior Engineer - AI ML at SQ1 Security Technology Pvt Ltd**, developing AI applications, API-integrated tools, and intelligent automation. Previously, I worked as a Machine Learning Engineer Intern at Vcodez and a Generative AI Developer Intern at Trueinfo Labs.

My work spans multi-agent evidence workflows, vulnerability management, medical NLP, clinical coding, and purpose-mapping and retrieval systems. I use Python, PyTorch, LangChain, and LangGraph to build and evaluate these solutions.
